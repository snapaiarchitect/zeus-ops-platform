# ZEUS OPS - Business Operations Manual

## Service Delivery Process

### Client Onboarding (48-Hour Sprint)

**Hour 0-4: Discovery**
- Welcome email with intake form
- Schedule kickoff call
- Access credentials shared securely

**Hour 4-24: Setup**
- Create client workspace
- Configure tools and integrations
- Review existing systems

**Hour 24-48: First Deliverable**
- Present AI opportunity assessment
- Define 30-day priorities
- Begin first implementation

### Weekly Client Rhythm

**Monday:**
- Weekly status email (automated template)
- Prioritize week's tasks

**Wednesday:**
- Mid-week check-in (15-min call or Loom)
- Adjust priorities if needed

**Friday:**
- Week summary report
- Hours logged and billed
- Next week's focus

### Service Tiers - What's Included

**Pilot ($2,500/mo):**
- 25 hours of AI operations support
- 1 discovery call/month
- Email support (24hr response)
- Weekly status reports
- Access to client dashboard

**Growth ($5,000/mo):**
- 50 hours of AI operations support
- 2 strategy calls/month
- Slack/Teams channel access
- Priority support (4hr response)
- Custom AI tool development
- Monthly performance reports

**Enterprise ($15,000/mo):**
- 150 hours (dedicated team)
- Daily standups

## Sales Process

### Lead Qualification (BANT)

**Budget:**
- Can they afford $2,500-$15,000/month?
- Is AI a priority in their budget?

**Authority:**
- Who makes the buying decision?
- Are we talking to the decision maker?

**Need:**
- Do they have a clear AI use case?
- Is the pain point urgent?

**Timeline:**
- When do they want to start?
- Is there a specific deadline?

### Discovery Call Script (30 min)

**0-5 min: Rapport & Agenda**
- Small talk
- "Here's what we'll cover today..."

**5-15 min: Current State**
- "Tell me about your current operations..."
- "Where are you using AI today?"
- "What's your biggest operational challenge?"

**15-25 min: Future State & Fit**
- "If you could wave a magic wand..."
- Present relevant case study
- Explain ZEUS OPS approach

**25-30 min: Next Steps**
- "Based on what we've discussed..."
- Propose pilot engagement
- Schedule follow-up

### Proposal Template Structure

1. **Executive Summary** (1 page)
   - Client's challenge
   - ZEUS OPS solution
   - Expected outcomes

2. **Scope of Work** (2-3 pages)
   - Specific deliverables
   - Timeline
   - Investment

3. **Case Studies** (1-2 pages)
   - Similar client success stories

4. **Terms & Conditions** (1 page)

## Risk Management

### Client Risks
- **Scope creep:** Clear SOWs, change orders
- **Payment delays:** Auto-charge, net 15 terms
- **Unrealistic expectations:** Set clear KPIs upfront

### Operational Risks
- **Key person dependency:** Document everything
- **Tool failures:** Have backups
- **Data security:** SOC 2 compliance path

### Financial Risks
- **Cash flow:** Maintain 3-month runway
- **Concentration:** No client >30% of revenue
- **Churn:** Monthly check-ins, NPS tracking
